## About

CS graduate from [UC Davis](https://cs.ucdavis.edu/) (2026), Technology Management minor. I live at the intersection of engineering, product, and client delivery — writing PRDs and production code on the same project.

A lot of that came through **[CodeLab Davis](https://www.codelabdavis.com/)**, where I spent two years embedded at companies like Reddit, Chevron, and Solidigm — eventually running the org as President, overseeing 110+ developers, designers, and PMs. I also got picked for **Deloitte OpX** (top 1% of interns), which gave me the chance to own a real internal AI product from a blank page to firm-wide handoff.

On the side, I tend to build the tool I wish existed — a self-hosted finance dashboard, a personal knowledge engine that runs through Claude's MCP, a way to help student orgs not lose institutional knowledge when leadership turns over. They address my needs and usually teach me something I couldn't learn any other way.

## Building

**seco** &nbsp;— A local professional identity engine built around Claude's MCP protocol. Capture experiences once through a native chat card or real-time voice (Deepgram / Whisper); render tailored copy for resumes, LinkedIn, GitHub, Overleaf, and cover letters. Your data stays on your machine.
`Node.js` `MCP SDK` `OpenAI` `Deepgram` `WebSockets` `SQLite` `Express`

**Heirloom** &nbsp;— Agentic knowledge continuity for student organizations. Ingests a club's Notion / Drive / Slack history, builds a temporal knowledge graph, and writes living handoff packages back into the workspace — solving leadership turnover at the org level.
`Python` `FastAPI` `LangGraph` `LangSmith` `Neo4j` `pgvector` `Redis` `Celery` `Next.js` `Clerk` `AWS S3`

**powerhour** &nbsp;— Self-hosted personal finance dashboard with two AI agents (24 tools), full Plaid integration, smart budget goals, and on-demand PDF report generation. Bring your own API keys; your financial data never leaves your server.
`Next.js` `PostgreSQL` `Drizzle ORM` `Gemini AI` `Plaid` `Docker` `PDFKit` `Tailwind CSS`

**dearfriends** &nbsp;— Collect mailing addresses from your network via a shareable link and keep real-world connections alive through physical letters — inspired by my mom's tradition of the Christmas card.
`Next.js` `Supabase` `Leaflet` `Resend` `Tailwind CSS`

**[Fathership](https://fathership.dev/)** &nbsp;— A free, anonymous space for young people without a father figure to ask the questions they can't ask anywhere else.
`Ruby on Rails` `PostgreSQL` `Tailwind CSS`
